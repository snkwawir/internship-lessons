# Corporate Esports Analytics & Strategic Engagement Research

## Overview
This project summarizes the analytical, research, and strategic evaluation work completed during an internship with the Corporate Esports Association (CEA), a national organization focused on workplace engagement through structured competitive gaming and charitable initiatives. The analysis combines internal tournament participation data, global esports benchmarking, game ecosystem evaluation, and employer engagement research to support long-term organizational planning and data-driven decision-making.

## Objectives
- Analyze multi-year esports participation and registration trends across internal and industry datasets
- Develop structured frameworks for evaluating esports ecosystem stability and game viability
- Benchmark organizational engagement performance against broader global esports trends
- Research the workplace, cognitive, and social benefits of esports participation
- Support strategic planning, operational reporting, and long-term data infrastructure initiatives

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- SQL
- Excel
- Data Analysis
- Statistical Modeling
- Strategic Analytics
- Research Synthesis
- Trend Forecasting
- Executive Reporting

## Key Features
- Analyzed internal CEA participation datasets alongside global tournament trend data to identify long-term engagement and ecosystem patterns
- Developed the 7-Signal Scoring Model, a structured evaluation framework used to classify esports titles based on community strength, publisher support, scalability, accessibility, and ecosystem stability
- Conducted comparative benchmarking analysis to identify participation declines exceeding broader industry contraction trends
- Produced executive-level reporting and strategic recommendations for product, engineering, and operations stakeholders
- Researched and synthesized academic and industry literature on workplace engagement, communication, collaboration, and employee development within esports environments
- Contributed to early-stage planning for a scalable internal data infrastructure and centralized reporting ecosystem

## Results
The analysis identified measurable engagement declines beyond broader industry contraction trends, helping leadership better understand operational and ecosystem-level challenges affecting tournament participation. The 7-Signal Scoring Model successfully differentiated stable esports ecosystems from higher-risk titles, improving strategic game evaluation and seasonal planning processes. The project also provided evidence-based insights into the organizational benefits of esports participation, supporting CEA’s long-term engagement and partnership initiatives.
